<!-- lang:en -->
First public Beta of StarLight: free and open source, under the GPL-3.0 license.
Open stacked or single images: FITS and XISF files, TIFF and PNG images and camera RAW formats.
Abracadabra prepares the image automatically (sky background, color, noise, stretch and finish), and every step stays adjustable by hand.
Calibrate and stack your frames: calibration frames, alignment, rejection of blurred frames, drizzle, HDR, mosaics, multiple nights and live sessions.
Processing tools and photographic filters, with local adjustments through masks.
Find out which objects are in the field with plate solving and downloadable deep-sky catalogs; add coordinates and a caption, and export for printing and social media.
Additional plugins and catalogs from the gallery, run in an isolated environment.
In Italian and English, with automatic updates from the Beta channel.
